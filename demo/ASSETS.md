# AETHER Demo Video Assets

## B-Roll Requirements

### Mechanical System Footage

**Required:**
1. **Double Pendulum** - The chaotic motion is visually striking and clearly demonstrates physics inference. Record from side view, 2-3 full swing cycles.
   - Duration needed: 15-20 seconds
   - Background: Clean, solid color or gradient
   - Lighting: Even, no harsh shadows
   - Preferred angle: 90 degrees perpendicular to motion plane

2. **Robot Arm** - Industrial or hobbyist arm performing repetitive task
   - Duration needed: 20-30 seconds
   - Should show clear joint articulation
   - Record from angle that shows all joints

3. **Slider-Crank Mechanism** - Simple mechanical system with clear constraints
   - Duration needed: 15 seconds
   - Shows constraint relationships well

4. **Bicycle Wheel** (spinning) - Demonstrates rotational dynamics
   - Duration needed: 10 seconds
   - High-speed rotation is visually appealing

**Sources:**
- Record real mechanisms (preferred)
- Wikimedia Commons: Search "double pendulum" or "four-bar linkage"
- Pexels.com: Royalty-free mechanical videos
- Coverr.co: Free video clips

**Recording Tips for B-Roll:**
- Use smartphone at 60fps or 120fps for slow-motion
- Mount on tripod - no handheld footage
- Leave 2 seconds of static lead-in/lead-out for editing
- Film in well-lit environment, avoid mixed lighting temperatures

### Screen Recording Footage

1. **AETHER Interface** - Clean, professional UI shots
   - Dark mode screenshot of dashboard
   - Upload zone in default state
   - Pipeline visualization running
   - Results panel with parameters
   - What-If Lab with sliders

2. **Simulation Overlay** - If available
   - Video playback with skeleton overlay
   - 3D trajectory visualization
   - Parameter fitting graphs

## Text Overlays

### Lower Thirds

**Style:** Dark semi-transparent bar, white text, left-aligned
**Font:** Inter Bold or SF Pro Display Bold
**Size:** ~32-36pt at 1080p
**Animation:** Fade in 200ms, hold 3-5 seconds, fade out 200ms

**Example text:**
```
THE PROBLEM
"CAD models are approximations. Physical testing is expensive."
```

```
THE SOLUTION
"AETHER extracts physics from any video in seconds."
```

## Typography

### Headlines/Titles
- **Font:** Inter Bold or SF Pro Display Bold
- **Size:** 48-72pt for main titles
- **Tracking:** Slight (0.02em) for modern feel

### Body/Parameters
- **Font:** Inter Regular or SF Pro Text
- **Size:** 24-32pt
- **Color:** White or #a1a1aa (muted)

### Monospace (for code/params)
- **Font:** JetBrains Mono or SF Mono
- **Size:** 20-28pt
- **Use:** Physics parameter values, confidence intervals
