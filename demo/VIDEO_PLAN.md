# AETHER Demo Video Plan

**Title:** "AETHER: Extract Physics from Any Video"

**Target Length:** 60-90 seconds

**Viral Hook (first 5 seconds):**
> "What if AI could watch a robot move and know its exact mass?"

**Core Value Proposition:**
> "Before: Hours of CAD modeling to estimate physics.
> After: 30 seconds of video and AETHER knows the mass, friction, and joint constraints."

---

## Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1: The Hook (0-10 seconds)

**Visual:** High-contrast black screen. A real mechanism appears: a robot arm or double pendulum in slow motion against clean white background. Minimalist. No UI visible yet.

**Voiceover:**
> "Every robot, every machine, every mechanism in your factory has hidden physics. Mass. Friction. Joint constraints. Traditionally, figuring this out took weeks of CAD modeling and physical testing."

**Visual Notes:**
- Show actual footage of a real mechanical system (pendulum, robot arm, or linkage mechanism)
- Use slow motion (0.25x or 0.5x) to emphasize the motion
- Clean, minimal aesthetic - no logos, no distractions
- Duration: 10 seconds

---

### Scene 2: The Problem (10-20 seconds)

**Visual:** Split screen or quick montage:
- Left: Engineer staring at CAD model, frustrated
- Center: Stack of spreadsheets with physics calculations
- Right: Physical prototype being tested, fail, iterate

**Voiceover:**
> "Today, getting these parameters right is a multi-week process. CAD models are approximations. Physical testing is expensive. And every guess is wrong."

**Visual Notes:**
- Show genuine frustration/problem-solving energy
- Keep it fast-paced (this is contrast for what comes next)
- Darker lighting to emphasize "before" state
- Duration: 10 seconds

---

### Scene 3: The Upload (20-30 seconds)

**Visual:** Hand drops a video file onto AETHER interface. The UI is clean, modern, dark theme with subtle purple/cyan accents. File icon animates as it "uploads."

**Voiceover:**
> "AETHER changes everything. Drop in a video of any mechanism. Watch AI extract the physics."

**Visual Notes:**
- Show the AETHER logo/interface clearly
- Use a real video file (pendulum, robot arm, bicycle, anything mechanical)
- Keep UI elements minimal - just show the upload zone
- Show progress indicator starting
- Duration: 10 seconds

---

### Scene 4: Pipeline Visualization (30-50 seconds)

**Visual:** Animated pipeline showing AETHER's processing stages. Each stage lights up sequentially:

1. **Frame Extraction** - Video frames appear in a strip
2. **Pose Estimation** - Skeleton overlay on mechanism
3. **Trajectory Analysis** - Paths drawn in 3D space
4. **Physics Inference** - Parameter values emerge and stabilize

**Voiceover:**
> "AETHER's pipeline analyzes every frame. Tracks motion. Infers masses, friction coefficients, joint limits. In seconds, you get physics parameters that match real-world behavior."

**Visual Notes:**
- Use a sleek, dark pipeline visualization with glowing nodes
- Each stage should take ~4-5 seconds to complete
- Show actual parameter values appearing (e.g., "Mass: 2.3kg", "Friction: 0.15")
- Keep camera steady - this is the "wow" technical moment
- Duration: 20 seconds

---

### Scene 5: Results Reveal (50-65 seconds)

**Visual:** Final physics parameters displayed on screen with clean typography. Show a comparison overlay: "Predicted vs Measured" with near-perfect match.

**Voiceover:**
> "The result: precise physics parameters extracted from video. Mass. Friction coefficients. Joint constraints. Ready for simulation, ready for control, ready for anything."

**Visual Notes:**
- Display parameters in large, readable typography
- Show confidence intervals
- Optional: show a side-by-side of video playback and synchronized simulation
- Build anticipation - this is the payoff
- Duration: 15 seconds

---

### Scene 6: What-If Lab (65-80 seconds)

**Visual:** AETHER's What-If Lab interface. User adjusts a slider for "Mass" - simulation updates in real-time showing the mechanism responding differently.

**Voiceover:**
> "But it gets better. Use AETHER's What-If Lab to explore. Change the mass. Adjust friction. See how your mechanism would behave before you build anything."

**Visual Notes:**
- Show a tangible, interactive slider
- Demonstrate before/after by changing one parameter
- Show the simulation responding in real-time
- Convey power and control
- Duration: 15 seconds

---

### Scene 7: The Closer (80-90 seconds)

**Visual:** Clean logo reveal. AETHER text with tagline. Website URL appears.

**Voiceover:**
> "Physics understanding from video. Open source. Try AETHER today at [website]."

**Visual Notes:**
- Keep it simple, confident, professional
- URL should be readable for ~5 seconds
- Optional: fade to black with "github.com/[repo]" subtle at bottom
- Duration: 10 seconds

## Technical Specifications

- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p) or 2560x1440 for crispness
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps (cinematic) or 60fps (if showing UI interactions)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Format:** ProRes 422 or H.264 for delivery
- **Color Grading:** Slight contrast boost, crushed blacks, subtle vignette
- **Typography:** SF Pro Display or Inter Bold for headlines, monospace for parameters

---

## Post-Production Notes

1. **Pacing:** Let moments breathe. The pipeline visualization should feel deliberate, not rushed.
2. **Music:** Start minimal (tension) → Build during processing → Confident resolution at end
3. **Transitions:** Use hard cuts between scenes 1-2, smooth fades within scenes 3-5
4. **Color Palette:**
   - Backgrounds: Near-black (#0a0a0f) to deep purple (#1a0a2e)
   - Accents: Electric cyan (#00d4ff) and violet (#8b5cf6)
   - Text: White with subtle glow
