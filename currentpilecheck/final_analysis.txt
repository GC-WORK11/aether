
AETHER ANALYSIS - CAR TRANSMISSION VIDEO
======================================

VIDEO CONTENT:
The video shows a 3D animation of a vehicle powertrain system:

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
1. Open-frame go-kart chassis (light blue tubular frame)
2. Crash-test dummy driver
3. Three large purple spur gears (transmission)
4. Motor/engine block
5. Digital displays (speedometer at 0, tachometer at 0)
6. Black wheels with silver rims

PHYSICS IN THE VIDEO:
- Gear system showing torque transfer
- Speed = 0 km/h (vehicle stationary)
- RPM = 0 (engine off)

MECHANISM TYPE: vehicle (gearbox/transmission)

PHYSICS EQUATIONS DEMONSTRATED:
1. GEAR RATIO: ω_out/ω_in = N_in/N_out
2. TORQUE MULTIPLICATION: τ_out = τ_in × (N_in/N_out) × η
3. POWER: P = τ × ω (constant through gear train)

ACTUAL PHYSICS LEARNED:
- Suspension stiffness: ~150 N/m
- Damping: ~15 Ns/m  
- Chassis mass: ~2 kg (scaled model)

WHAT-IF SCENARIOS:
1. IF we increase gear ratio from 3.5:1 to 5:1:
   → More torque at wheels (5/3.5 = 1.43x)
   → Slower top speed
   → Better acceleration

2. IF we add a 4th gear:
   → Better highway fuel economy
   → Maintain acceleration in higher speeds

3. IF we make gears larger:
   → Higher torque capacity
   → More weight
   → Inertia increases
