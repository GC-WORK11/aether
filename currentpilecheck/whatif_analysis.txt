
WHAT-IF: Car Transmission Analysis
==================================

Based on the car engine and transmission video:

SCENARIO: If gear ratio changes from 3.5:1 to 2.0:1

PHYSICS:
1. Torque multiplication: (3.5/2.0) = 1.75x MORE torque at wheels
2. Engine RPM at 60mph: Would DROP from ~3000 to ~1700 RPM
3. Fuel efficiency: Would IMPROVE by ~30% due to lower RPM
4. Acceleration: Would SLOW initially but sustain higher top speed

REAL EQUATIONS:
- Gear Ratio = Engine RPM / Wheel RPM
- Wheel Torque = Engine Torque × Gear Ratio × Efficiency
- Efficiency typical: 85-95% for manual, 75-85% for automatic

SIMULATION NEEDED:
Run MuJoCo with:
- Lower gear ratio (2.0:1)
- Same engine torque curve
- Measure: 0-60 time, top speed, fuel consumption
